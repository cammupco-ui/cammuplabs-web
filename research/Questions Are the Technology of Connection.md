# Evidence Brief for CAMMUPLABS: "Questions Are the Technology of Connection"

## TL;DR
- **The thesis is empirically defensible.** Two decades of peer-reviewed research — from Arthur Aron's 36-Questions procedure (1997) to Karen Huang et al.'s Harvard work on question-asking and liking (*Journal of Personality and Social Psychology*, 2017) to DeepMind's "Habermas Machine" in *Science* (Tessler, Bakker et al., 2024) — converge on the finding that carefully designed questions reliably produce closeness, learning, and democratic common ground, while statement-only or "telling" interaction does not.
- **All three product stages sit on real, growing markets with explicit policy tailwinds.** The global mental-health-apps market is ~US$7-8.5B in 2024-25 growing at ~15% CAGR (Roots Analysis; Grand View; MarketsandMarkets) for Stage 1. UK has invested £80M+ since 2018, Japan launched a Cabinet-level Loneliness Headquarters with a 2023 Act, and Seoul alone has committed ₩451.3 billion (~US$330M over five years) for Stage 3.
- **Recommended investor framing**: position CAMMUPLABS not as a "conversation app" but as **the first company building AI mediation infrastructure** — software (and later hardware) that sits *between* humans rather than replacing them. This is the architectural opposite of Sherry Turkle's critique of AI companionship, and it is the exact insight DeepMind validated in *Science* with the Habermas Machine.

## 0. THE SCIENCE OF QUESTIONS (cross-cutting)

### 0.1 Question-asking, not conversation in general, drives liking
**Huang, K., Yeomans, M., Brooks, A.W., Minson, J., & Gino, F. (2017). "It Doesn't Hurt to Ask: Question-Asking Increases Liking." *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology*, 113(3), 430–452.** Across three studies of live dyadic conversations (online get-to-know-you chats and a field study of 110 in-person speed daters), people who asked more questions — particularly **follow-up questions** — were rated as significantly more likable. A trained NLP "follow-up question detector" showed speed daters who asked more follow-ups were significantly more likely to elicit second-date agreement. People do *not* anticipate this effect. Verbatim: *"People who ask more questions, particularly follow-up questions, are better liked by their conversation partners. … Despite the persistent and beneficial effects of asking questions, people do not anticipate that question-asking increases interpersonal liking."* A 2019 reply by Yeomans et al. (*JPSP*) defends the result against critique; a 2025 published correction (*JPSP*, PMID 40111841) leaves the conclusions intact.

**Why it matters for CAMMUPLABS.** This is the empirical core. CAMMUPLABS's product is not "more conversation"; it is more *question-asking*, especially follow-ups. That distinction is what makes the AI-as-mediator role coherent.

### 0.2 The Aron procedure — closeness engineered with 45 minutes of escalating questions
**Aron, A., Melinat, E., Aron, E.N., Vallone, R.D., & Bator, R.J. (1997). "The Experimental Generation of Interpersonal Closeness: A Procedure and Some Preliminary Findings." *Personality and Social Psychology Bulletin*, 23(4), 363–377.** Strangers exchanging 36 escalating self-disclosure questions over 45 minutes reported significantly higher closeness than strangers given small-talk prompts of equal duration. The procedure has been used in hundreds of replication and extension studies (UC Office of the President). A German replication by Sparrer & Wesche found the protocol increased social integration among undergraduate freshmen *regardless* of migration status, disability, age, or sexual orientation — i.e., it travels across difference (UC Berkeley Greater Good in Action).

**Why it matters.** This is the historical and methodological backbone of TalkBingo. It also shows that *structure* — escalation, reciprocity, follow-up — is what makes questions work. An AI mediator can enforce that structure at scale.

### 0.3 Humble Inquiry — the formal frame for question-based leadership
**Schein, E.H. (2013). *Humble Inquiry: The Gentle Art of Asking Instead of Telling*. Berrett-Koehler.** Late MIT Sloan emeritus Edgar Schein defined Humble Inquiry as *"the fine art of drawing someone out, of asking questions to which you do not already know the answer, of building a relationship based on curiosity and interest in the other person."* Schein argued that organizations and relationships fail because of a "culture of Tell" and that question-based interaction is the corrective. This is the executive-coaching frame for CAMMUPLABS's B2B narratives.

### 0.4 AI mediation — the emerging peer-reviewed category
**Tessler, M.H., Bakker, M.A., Jarrett, D., Sheahan, H., Chadwick, M.J., Koster, R., Evans, G., Campbell-Gillingham, L., Collins, T., Parkes, D.C., Botvinick, M., & Summerfield, C. (2024). "AI can help humans find common ground in democratic deliberation." *Science*.** Google DeepMind, Oxford, Harvard, and Yale built the "Habermas Machine," an LLM-based **caucus mediator**, and tested it with 5,700+ UK participants in groups discussing contentious political issues. Findings: (1) AI-mediated group statements were rated clearer, more informative, and less biased than statements from trained human mediators; (2) participants' positions converged when mediated by the AI but remained polarized in direct debate; (3) the AI fairly represented majority and minority viewpoints; (4) the effect replicated in a demographically representative UK sample. Quote from Michael Henry Tessler (DeepMind): *"The large language model was trained to identify and present areas of overlap between the ideas held among group members. It was not trained to be persuasive but to act as a mediator."* The dataset and code are public at github.com/google-deepmind/habermas_machine.

**Why it matters.** This is the single most important new citation for CAMMUPLABS. It is **peer-reviewed evidence in *Science*** that AI can mediate human-to-human dialogue better than untrained human facilitators. CAMMUPLABS is operationalizing the same architectural insight at consumer scale and in education — with *questions* (rather than summaries) as the core primitive.

### 0.5 The counter-thesis CAMMUPLABS rebuts: AI as substitute
**Turkle, S. (2015, updated 2025). *Reclaiming Conversation: The Power of Talk in a Digital Age*. Penguin.** Turkle, the Abby Rockefeller Mauzé Professor of the Social Studies of Science and Technology at MIT, is the canonical critic of AI as conversation substitute. In her 2025 preface she describes the seduction of GPT-4o's empathic voice — *"Sherry, that is very hard. Be sure you are taking care of yourself"* — and worries the tool will displace human interlocutors. Jonathan Haidt has called *Reclaiming Conversation* "a landmark work of media scholarship." Turkle's iconic phrase: *"We have sacrificed conversation for mere connection. … We are forever elsewhere."*

**Why it matters.** CAMMUPLABS's thesis is the exact inverse of what Turkle critiques. The AI does not pretend to be a companion; it sits *between* humans and gives them better questions. This positioning is rhetorically powerful with cautious investors, school boards, and senior-care procurement officers.

### 0.6 Perspective-taking and deep canvassing
- **Broockman, D. & Kalla, J. (2016). "Durably reducing transphobia: A field experiment on door-to-door canvassing." *Science*.** A single ~10-minute deep-canvassing conversation, structured around questions inviting analogous personal experience, reduced anti-transgender prejudice in approximately 1 in 10 voters, with effects persisting at 3 months — magnitude comparable to the entire decline in anti-gay prejudice between 1998 and 2012.
- **Kalla, J. & Broockman, D. (2020). "Reducing Exclusionary Attitudes through Interpersonal Conversation: Evidence from Three Field Experiments." *American Political Science Review*.** Replicates and extends to immigration attitudes (4-point shift) across CA and TN, with naive canvassers from seven unaffiliated organizations.
- **People's Action 2020 Biden phone-canvassing field experiment (Broockman & Kalla):** deep canvassing produced a statistically significant **3.1-point swing (p=0.03) in Biden vote margin per 100 completed calls**; 4.9 points among women, 6.7 among independents, 8.5 among independent women.

**Why it matters.** Strongest available evidence that *question-based, non-judgmental, story-eliciting dialogue* changes minds — directly relevant to Stages 2 (debate/discussion) and 3 (intergenerational and civic spaces).

## 1. STAGE 1 (B2C — TalkBingo): EVERYDAY DEPTH IN FAMILIES, COUPLES, FRIENDS

## 2. STAGE 2 (B2B — EDUCATION DISCUSSION/DEBATE PLATFORM)

### 2.1 Academic evidence — structured dialogue and questioning improve learning
- **Hattie, J. (2009, updated through 2018+). *Visible Learning*.** Synthesis of 1,200+ meta-analyses, ~300M students. **"Classroom discussion" effect size d ≈ 0.82**, well above Hattie's d = 0.40 "hinge point" representing one year of additional growth; among Hattie's top 15 of 256 ranked influences. (Caveat: Matthew A. Kraft (Brown) has noted in *Educational Leadership* that some Visible Learning rankings rest on correlational rather than causal evidence; use as directional.)
- **Philosophy for Children (P4C / Matthew Lipman, 1970s onward).**
  - **Yan, S., Walters, L.M., Wang, Z., & Wang, C.-C. (2018). "Meta-analysis of the Effectiveness of Philosophy for Children Programs on Students' Cognitive Outcomes." *Analytic Teaching and Philosophical Praxis*, 39(1).** Overall **d = 0.58** on cognitive outcomes; **d = 1.06 on reasoning skill** specifically (10 studies, n = 1,509).
  - **Three-level meta-analysis (Liu et al., 2025, PMC12565533).** g = 0.59 across 33 studies (n = 4,568), robust across grade levels; strongest effects for reasoning, critical thinking, and creativity.
  - **UK Education Endowment Foundation (EEF) trial of P4C (Gorard, Siddiqui, See 2015).** +2 months reading and maths progress; **+4 months reading and +3 months maths for Free-School-Meal pupils** — P4C is equity-positive.
- **Dialogic teaching — Robin Alexander (Cambridge), EEF RCT (Jay et al. 2017).** ~2 months additional progress in English, maths, and science for 4,600 Year 5 pupils.
- **Accountable Talk — Lauren Resnick (University of Pittsburgh; Institute for Learning).** Framework for structured, evidence-based classroom discussion adopted across US districts.
- **Harkness method — Phillips Exeter Academy.** Seminar-style oval-table learning; widely imitated in elite secondary education.

### 2.2 SEL market and policy tailwinds — the demand-side case
- **CASEL & RAND (2024). *Social and Emotional Learning in U.S. Schools* (2023-24 school year).** Headline numbers:
  - **83% of K-12 principals report their school used an SEL curriculum in 2023-24**, up from 76% in 2021-22 and **46% in 2017-18 — a record high adoption rate**.
  - **49 U.S. states + DC have at least one supportive SEL policy.**
  - **27 states have adopted freestanding K-12 SEL competencies/standards** (CASEL 2022 State Scan); all 50 states have pre-K SEL standards.
  - **Caveat:** Three states — **Arizona, Indiana, and Iowa — removed their freestanding K-12 SEL standards** between 2022 and 2024 (RAND/CASEL).
  - **CASEL 2024 SEL Exchange** drew 1,800+ attendees from 30 countries.
- **U.S. Surgeon General (2023). *Our Epidemic of Loneliness and Isolation*.** Explicitly directs schools to "develop a strategic plan for building social connectedness and social skills, including implementing peer-led programs" — federal-level cover for structured-dialogue procurement.
- **Korean policy — 2022 Revised National Curriculum (2022 개정 교육과정).** Ministry of Education (교육부) Notice No. 2022-33 (December 22, 2022) makes 토의·토론 학습 (discussion-and-debate learning) a required pedagogical mode and elevates **"비판적 사고" (critical thinking), "협력적 소통 역량" (collaborative communication competency), and "공동체 역량" (community competency) to core competencies (핵심역량)**. Direct quote from the General Provisions: *"학교는 … 토의·토론 학습을 통해 자신의 생각을 표현하는 기회를 가질 수 있도록 한다."* For the 자유학기 (Free Semester): *"토의·토론 학습, 프로젝트 학습 등 학생 참여형 수업을 강화."* Rollout: 2024 (Grades 1-2) → 2025 (Gr 3-4, M1, H1) → 2026 (Gr 5-6, M2, H2) → 2027 full implementation. MOE press release Nov 24, 2021 framed the reform as *"학습량 적정화, 비판적 사고 함양 및 탐구 중심으로 교수·학습과 평가 개선."*
- **자유학기제 effectiveness — Park, Y. (KDI/SSRN, 2017).** Free-Semester implementation "increased the frequency of classroom discussions, group projects, and extra-curricular activities" and improved students' sociality (close-friend counts), particularly through teacher adoption of student-to-student interaction.

**Why it matters.** Korea's Ministry of Education has *mandated* the exact pedagogical mode (토의·토론) that CAMMUPLABS's Stage 2 product scaffolds. Teachers need tools to comply; many have not been trained in dialogic methods. This is a *policy-aligned* B2B market with a defined nationwide phase-in to 2027.

## 3. STAGE 3 (PUBLIC / COMMUNITY HARDWARE)

### 3.1 Intergenerational programs work
- **Campbell Systematic Reviews (2024, Wiley).** Intergenerational activity reduces loneliness and exclusion for both older and younger participants, improves mental health, and tackles ageism.
- **Impact of Intergenerational Bonding Program (PMC12645515, 2024).** Meta-analysis of 4 trials (n = 234): significant reductions in loneliness and depression; improvements in life satisfaction in older adults.
- **Zhang, Xi & Weiss (2023). "Intergenerational Contact and the Benefits for Older Adults." *Innovation in Aging* (NIH PMC10735672).** Reviews mechanisms: reduced loneliness, lower stress, greater sense of purpose, reduced depression risk, improved cross-generational attitudes.
- **Korean 경로당 ecosystem (한국 자료).** **Ministry of Health and Welfare (보건복지부), "2024 노인복지시설 현황" (June 30, 2024)**: 70,455 senior leisure-welfare facilities (노인복지관 + 경로당 + 노인교실) in 2023, including ~68,180 경로당 at end-2022 (MOHW 2023 시설 현황, 백세시대). 2024 MOHW budget for 경로당 cooling/heating/grain subsidy alone: **₩77.3 billion** (up from ₩71.5B in 2023). May 1, 2024 MOHW press release announces 5-day-per-week meals-program expansion across nationwide 경로당 (described as "전국 경로당 (6.9만 개)").

### 3.2 Third places and social infrastructure
- **Oldenburg, R. (1989). *The Great Good Place*.** Coined "third place" — informal public gathering spaces distinct from home and work. Foundational text for community-design pitches.
- **Klinenberg, E. (2018). *Palaces for the People: How Social Infrastructure Can Help Fight Inequality, Polarization, and the Decline of Civic Life*.** NYU sociologist Klinenberg argues physical "social infrastructure" — libraries, parks, community centers — is a critical predictor of community resilience. Libraries are reframed as core civic infrastructure for connection.

**Why it matters.** Klinenberg's framework is precisely the intellectual positioning for Stage 3 hardware: CAMMUPLABS would not be selling "tablets for old people"; it would be selling **social infrastructure equipment**, a category libraries, kominkan, and 경로당 already buy.

### 3.3 Public-policy spending — the loneliness budgets
- **United Kingdom.** The world's first national Loneliness Strategy launched October 2018 (Theresa May) following the Jo Cox Commission. **The UK Government and partners have invested more than £80 million** since 2018 (DCMS Tackling Loneliness Hub, 2023 annual update). UK Budget 2023 added a further £100M for charities and community organizations. Over 1.3 million social-prescribing referrals as of November 2022 (NHS England).
- **United States.** 2023 U.S. Surgeon General Vivek Murthy advisory, *Our Epidemic of Loneliness and Isolation*, established the **National Strategy to Advance Social Connection** with six pillars (first: "strengthen social infrastructure"). The primary academic estimate that social isolation among older adults drives ~**US$6.7 billion in excess Medicare spending annually** traces to **Shaw, Farid, Noel-Miller et al. (2017), *Journal of Aging and Health*, DOI 10.1177/0898264317703559** (AARP Public Policy Institute / Stanford), quoted as: *"a lack of social contacts among older adults is associated with an estimated $6.7 billion in additional Medicare spending annually."* The Surgeon General cites Holt-Lunstad et al. (2010) for the "premature death comparable to smoking 15 cigarettes a day" formulation.
- **Japan.** **Tetsushi Sakamoto (坂本哲志) appointed the world's second Minister of Loneliness and Isolation (孤独・孤立対策担当大臣) on February 12, 2021** by PM Suga. **¥6 billion (≈US$55M) emergency budget for NPOs working on loneliness/isolation, March 2021** (PM's Office statement Sept 27, 2021: *"In March, we implemented emergency measures worth 6 billion yen to support non-profit organizations (NPOs) and other groups"*). The **Act on the Advancement of Measures to Address Loneliness and Isolation (孤独・孤立対策推進法, Act No. 45 of 2023)** took effect **April 1, 2024**, creating a PM-chaired Headquarters with all cabinet ministers as members (Cabinet Secretariat). The FY2025 Cabinet Office tracking compilation lists **143 line-items across ministries**, including ¥8.3B for the 地域少子化対策重点推進交付金 and ¥2.4B for the 社会参加活躍支援等孤独・孤立対策推進交付金. **2024 Cabinet Office annual survey: 39.3% of Japanese felt lonely; 4.3% always or frequently. Estimated 22,000 solitary deaths (孤独死) per year. 1.46 million hikikomori (~2% of population)** (Cabinet Office 2022/2023 surveys; Japan Times June 2025).
- **Korea — 외로움 없는 서울 (Loneliness-Free Seoul).** Mayor Oh Se-hoon announced **October 21, 2024** the nation's first comprehensive municipal anti-loneliness plan with a budget of **₩451.3 billion (~US$330 million) over five years (2024-2028)**, broken down as ₩52.6B (2024) → ₩69.1B (2025) → ₩115.7B (2027) → ₩119.1B (2028) (Kookmin Ilbo Oct 21, 2024; Seoul Mediahub policy archive). Seven flagship programs include the "외로움안녕 120" 24/7 hotline (launches April 2025), 서울마음편의점 (4 pilot "mind convenience-stores" in 2025), 365 Seoul Challenge, and a planned 고립예방센터 (isolation-prevention center). Backdrop figures cited in the Seoul press release: **62.1% of Seoul's single-person households report loneliness (2023); ~130,000 socially isolated youth in Seoul alone**.
- **Korea — national level.** MOHW released the first 고립·은둔 청년 지원방안 December 13, 2023; 청년미래센터 pilots launched July 2024 in Incheon, Ulsan, Chungbuk, and Jeonbuk.

### 3.4 Stage 3 — the bottom line
There is **roughly US$500M+/year in committed public spend across UK, US, Japan, and Korea on loneliness and social connection**, with explicit policy direction toward "social infrastructure" and community spaces. Korea's ~68,000 경로당, Japan's kominkan, Anglo-American library networks, and U.S. community centers are all viable distribution channels for question-based mediation hardware.

## 7. CAVEATS

- **Effect-size interpretation.** Hattie's d ≈ 0.82 for "classroom discussion" is partly correlational; Matthew A. Kraft of Brown has warned in *Educational Leadership* that some Visible Learning rankings reflect selection rather than causation. Use as directional rather than precision evidence.
- **CASEL is contested.** Arizona, Indiana, and Iowa removed freestanding K-12 SEL standards between 2022 and 2024. SEL framing can be politically polarizing in some U.S. red-state contexts. Korean ("사회정서학습," "협력적 소통 역량") and Canadian framings are less politically contested.
- **Gottman's "90%" statistic** appears in Gottman Institute marketing rather than as a peer-reviewed effect size; the underlying research (esp. Shapiro, Gottman & Carrere 2000 in *Journal of Family Psychology* 14(1)) is solid but cite as "Gottman Institute" for the popularized framing.
- **Loneliness budgets are fragmented.** Japan's loneliness spending is distributed across 143 line-items in multiple ministries; comparing the ¥6B 2021 emergency package to Seoul's ₩451.3B/5y multi-year commitment is not apples-to-apples. Always specify scope (emergency vs. multi-year vs. annual operating).
- **The Habermas Machine** mediated *summaries*, not *questions*. CAMMUPLABS's claim that AI question-mediation works generalizes from this evidence + Huang 2017 — it is not a direct replication. A dedicated CAMMUPLABS efficacy study (pre/post Inclusion-of-Other-in-Self scale, RCT-style) will materially strengthen the science deck.
- **Sherry Turkle would likely object** even to question-based AI mediation if she felt it displaced human effort. Address this openly: questions sit *between* humans rather than *replacing* a human interlocutor — the AI generates the prompt; the humans do the talking.
- **Seoul ₩451.3B figure** is materially larger than the ~US$66M figure that has circulated in some Korean press summaries. The ₩451.3 billion / 5-year number is the official Seoul Metropolitan Government press release figure (Oct 21, 2024) and is what should be cited.
- **CAMMUPLABS-specific differentiation must be defended.** The competitive case rests on three claims: (a) follow-up question generation (Huang) is the critical AI capability, (b) Korean dialogic-education policy creates a near-term mandated buying clock, (c) Klinenberg-framed "social infrastructure" reframes Stage 3 hardware away from companion-robot critiques. Each of these should be tested in customer interviews before committing capital.
