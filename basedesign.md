company researching "question technology" for human communication. 
Desktop 1440×900. Snap-scroll layout — 7 full-screen sections, each 
exactly one viewport tall.

═══════════════════════════════════════
COMPANY POSITIONING
═══════════════════════════════════════
- Name: CAMMUP (legal: CAMMUPLABS)
- Tagline: "We build the technology of questions."
- Korean sub: "질문이 기술이 되는 곳"

- Tone: confident, professional, restrained warmth — NOT cute or

VISUAL SYSTEM
═══════════════════════════════════════

- Accent 1: brand pink #FF0051 (used sparingly, for emphasis)
- Accent 2: ink navy #1E2A44 (professional weight)
- Soft pastels (peach, sage, sky) used only as subtle background 
  blobs, not as primary surfaces
- Typography: Anta for headlines (geometric, technical), Inter for 
  body, Alexandria for Korean
- Generous whitespace, editorial layout, data-forward feel
- Each section may have a small section number (01, 02, ...) and 
  right-side snap indicator dots

LAYOUT NOTES
═══════════════════════════════════════
- Each section is exactly 1440×900, fills the viewport
- Consistent nav at top of every section (sticky)
- Right edge of every section: small vertical snap dots indicator 
  (7 dots, current section highlighted in pink)
- Generous padding (80-120px sides)
- Korean and English copy coexist — English is the primary 
  headline weight, Korean is secondary in muted color
- No stock photos, no illustrations of people — use abstract 
  geometric shapes, data visualizations, and typography as the 
  primary visual elements
